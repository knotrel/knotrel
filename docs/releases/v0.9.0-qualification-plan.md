# v0.9.0 final performance campaign: plan (roadmap 0.9.6)

**Status: PROPOSED, awaiting maintainer approval. Nothing has been measured.**
Once approved, references, cells, thresholds, budget and the decision rule do
not change; the detailed protocol is frozen in knotrel-benchmarks before
collection, as for v0.7.0. A failed cell is reported as failed and is not
reinterpreted.

## Why and what

v0.8.0 was released without a qualification (decision D2), so the latest
qualified comparison is v0.7.0 against v0.6.0. The roadmap asks v0.9.0 for one
proportionate final campaign, with criteria and budget fixed beforehand and no
open-ended reruns.

What changed in the server code since v0.7.0 and could affect performance:
- v0.8.0: opt-in inspection routes and node index (off by default; their cost
  with inspection on was checked separately,
  [console-inspection-costs.md](../console-inspection-costs.md)), core
  neighbor listing (opt-in), `fixedbitset` 0.5.7 → 0.5.9;
- v0.9.0: reserved admission lanes for feeder requests (0.9.3), an additive
  `/v1/info` field. Everything else in v0.9.0 is outside the server
  (feeder, examples, CI, documentation).

## Builds

- **Candidate:** the v0.9.0 release candidate commit on `main` after the
  version bump, release build. The release commit's `crates/`, `Cargo.toml`
  and `Cargo.lock` must equal the measured candidate's, or the campaign is
  repeated.
- **Deciding reference:** published **v0.7.0**, the last qualified release.
- **Descriptive reference:** published **v0.8.0**, the version users run.

## Experiments

**Part 1: deciding, candidate against v0.7.0.** The v0.7.0 qualification-3
campaign (knotrel-benchmarks `results/v0.7.0-qualification-3/`) re-run
unchanged in method: experiments A, B1–B5, C1–C4, T5, R, Rmem, M_desc and
M_reg, with their cells, pair counts, estimators, seeds, workloads,
conformance rules (host-interference gate, unit repetition at most twice)
and **every threshold unchanged** (A primary 10 %, query tail 25 %, RSS 20 %,
the B and C cell tolerances, T5 25 %, R, Rmem and M_reg tolerances, the
metrics budget 6 %). Only the binaries and the campaign directory change.

**Part 2: descriptive, candidate against v0.8.0.** The cells that touch what
changed after v0.8.0: M_reg (queries with concurrent feeder events) and the
B4 feeder-events cells. Reported with the same estimators, no verdict.

**Part 3: feeder under load on the candidate.** The approved
[feeder-under-load](../integrations/feeder-under-load.md) protocol, all cells,
3 observations, criteria Q1–Q5 unchanged. Q3 is a declared limitation of
v0.9.0 and is reported again as measured; Q1, Q2, Q4 and Q5 decide.

## Decision rule

- **Part 1** classifies every cell PASS, INCONCLUSIVE, REGRESSION, FAIL or
  EXCEEDS_BUDGET (disclosure categories as in v0.7.0). v0.9.0 is
  **qualified** only if every deciding cell is PASS (disclosure categories
  reported as such). Any other result is reported as it is, for a maintainer
  decision; **no exception from v0.7.0 or earlier carries over**, and the
  v0.7.0 exceptions (three INCONCLUSIVE cells, the `hub` 10k lifecycle cells)
  are measured again and classified anew.
- **Part 2** is descriptive: it states the change from v0.8.0, never a pass.
- **Part 3:** Q1, Q2, Q4 and Q5 must pass as they did for change A; Q3 stays
  FAIL as declared, with the new numbers.
- No performance claim beyond what the classification supports; the release
  notes report the outcome with its limits.

## Environment and budget

- The same Apple M3 host, macOS, AC power, the frozen host-interference gate
  of v0.7.0; nothing else running (checked before and during).
- **Budget:** Part 1 at most 105 minutes per attempt, as in v0.7.0; Part 2 at
  most 20 minutes; Part 3 at most 30 minutes. About 2 h 35 in all.
- **Attempts:** one. A second attempt only if the first does not conform
  (gate, budget or recovery exhausted), never because of a result; a second
  non-conforming attempt ends the campaign for a maintainer decision.
- Raw data, freeze files and the decision record are stored in
  knotrel-benchmarks with checksums, as for v0.7.0.

## Order

1. Version bump to 0.9.0 and the release candidate on `main`.
2. knotrel-benchmarks: protocol frozen from this plan (binaries, campaign

3. Parts 1, 2 and 3, in that order, in one session.
4. Classification, decision record, release notes and
   [performance by version](../performance-by-version.md).
